# Phase 06 Context: Learning Sites Template

## Requirements (Source: Wireframe Image)
The Learning Site detail page must follow a strict editorial layout:

1. **Hero Section**:
   - Primary Headline (H1): "Learning Site name here"
   - Metadata Sidebar: Founded, Location, Learning Site Category, Lead Partners.
   - Large wide image placeholder + smaller accent image.

2. **Challenges Section**:
   - Narrative block explaining site-specific difficulties.
   - Large vertical image placeholder on the right.

3. **Projects & Initiatives**:
   - Grid or row of initiative cards.
   - Focus on "Ecological & Economic Renewal".

4. **Site-specific Market Invention Strategies**:
   - Detailed section for regional economic innovations.

5. **Ecological & Community Impact Data**:
   - High-contrast stats section.
   - Metrics like "Tree Survival Rate", "Income Increase", "Carbon Sequestration".
   - Mixed layout with small images and large numbers.

6. **Future Goals**:
   - Italicized, narrative section.

7. **Image Gallery**:
   - Minimalist grid.

8. **Navigation Integration**:
   - Must be accessible from the Homepage "Explore Our Sites" carousel.

## Design Constraints
- **Aesthetic**: "Clinical Luxury" (Botanical, modern, digitally crisp).
- **Typography**: BioRhyme (Serif) for headers, Inter/Space Grotesk (Sans/Mono) for metadata.
- **Palette**: Neon Lime (#DBFF66), Viridian (#006F53), Night Forest (#06211A), Platinum (#F5F5F5).
- **Motion**: `KineticReveal` for headers, `ScrollGlow` for background depth, `Magnetic` for interactive cards.
