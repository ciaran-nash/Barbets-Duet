# Phase 2 Context: Barbet's Philosophy & History

## Phase Goal
Create a high-fidelity, narrative-driven page at `/about/philosophy-history` that communicates the intellectual depth and historical origins of the collective.

## The Narrative (Context)
- **Origins:** Evolved from SID scenarios (1998-2008). Formally initiated in 2009 at the "Invention Convention" in Tanzania.
- **The Jumuiya:** Adopted in 2014 (Lukenya Convention) to describe a constellation of learning sites.
- **The Experiment:** A transition from a "20-year experiment" to a multi-generational effort to solve the **Oak Tree Paradox** (valuing life vs. destruction).

## Philosophical Pillars (The System)
1. **The Unsynchronised Duet:** Cross-cultural collaboration (Crested Barbet metaphor). Distinct voices singing together on equal terms.
2. **Oak Tree Paradox:** Business idea, not a charity. Inventing markets that reward life.
3. **Mosaic vs. Column Rights:** Fences (exclusive) vs. Footpaths (inclusive). High social equity + high biodiversity.
4. **Trial & Error:** A "Living Archive" of mistakes. Radical honesty prompts: "What have you tried? What made you laugh?"

## Visual Strategy (Wireframe Alignment)
- **Hero:** Focus on "Restoring Livelihoods and Land."
- **Origins Section:** Use a **Year Grid** (1998, 2006, 2009, 2014) instead of a simple timeline.
- **Philosophy Section:** "A System for Restoration" - 3-column pillar grid with "Clinical Luxury" iconography.
- **Trial & Error Section:** A "Living Archive" block featuring the radical honesty prompts.

## Implementation Pattern
- **Route:** `/about/philosophy-history`
- **Components:** `PhilosophyHistoryContent.tsx` with modular sections.
- **Aesthetic:** BioRhyme (Serif) for philosophical weight, JetBrains Mono for system labels. Grayscale-to-color hover transitions.
